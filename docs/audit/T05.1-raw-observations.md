## Delivered

`packages/movement/movement/observations.py`: pure, typed internal per-frame
observations for elbow and upper-arm/torso interior angles. Projected2D scales XY
by upright source dimensions; estimated3D uses separately qualified XYZ. Anatomical
side is explicit. Missing/invalid/interpolated joints and degenerate geometry yield
null reasons per metric, with global deterministic reason priority. No gap filling,
cross-arm averaging, automatic side selection, rep counting or activation claims.

This is a diagnostic building block. Existing movement/core rep/metric scaffolds,
exercise configs/thresholds, public contracts/API reports and scoring are unchanged.

Protocol: `../measurement/T05.1-protocol.md`. Blank rep/angle CSV headers live under
its templates directory; none contains human labels. Reference guidance and demo-link
shortlist: `../measurement/T06.1-reference-plan.md`. User can ask a reviewer to assess
our shortlisted videos; supplying a trainer-recorded demonstration is not required.
External demos have not been played, downloaded, approved or used as trajectories.
