# HM04.1 — experimental reference assessment in the app

22 October2026. Owner-authorized personal prototype, not validated technique scoring.

The existing0.3 movement endpoint now applies a separate deterministic experimental
comparison after measurement and before the existing owner/retention recheck. The
main app displays the estimate, reference bands, signed differences and formula.
Legacy0.2 pose reports and the frozen `lat-component-policy-v1`/trusted scoring engine
are unchanged. No approval status or reviewer was fabricated. D017 records the choice.

## Actual method

- Fixed primary QMotion, following owner preference, with four historical reference
  cycles. Reference-only bands are copied exactly from `T05.2c-rep-review.json`.
  Catalyst and IMSA stay separate in configuration, not pooled or chosen opportunistically.
- Compare torso-axis excursion (camera plane), elbow excursion and return extension
  (estimated3D), and upper-arm/torso angle at bottom (estimated3D). Bands are observed
  min/max in reference footage, not acceptable technique ranges.
- For value x and band[L,U], distance=max(L-x,0,x-U); resemblance=
  100*max(0,1-distance/30). **30degrees is a new, unreviewed engineering normalization
  proposal**, not an empirically calibrated or owner-reviewed tolerance.
- Average the two elbow component resemblances into arm-ROM. Average arm-ROM,
  torso-dynamic and shoulder-drive families equally per cycle, then average eligible
  completed cycles equally. All four values must be observed with at least80% supporting
  coverage, correct side, unit and dimensionality. Incomplete/missing cycles are excluded;
  no eligible cycles yields null. Zero resemblance and zero deviation remain real zeros.
- Every numeric estimate is `partial`, including100. Absolute gravity-calibrated torso
  start/backward lean and bar/chest endpoint are omitted. Camera compatibility and
  normalization remain unreviewed. Top-level technique `score` remains null; the new
  number lives only in `prototype_assessment.estimated_match`.
