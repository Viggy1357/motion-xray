## What the computation means

`movement.review_cycles` uses explicit frozen thresholds from
`data/review_configs/lat-reference-v1.json`: estimated3D elbow low100°, high135°,
dwell0.1s, max gap0.1s. These are engineering trial assumptions, not human-reviewed
excursion calibration or accepted technique ranges. Missing samples reset candidates;
no per-candidate normalization or filled gaps create events. Confirmed high/low/high
dwell uses original timestamps. Reported bottom is low-threshold entry, not a measured
kinematic extremum; reported phase durations inherit that limitation.

`scoring.review_comparison` uses one shared phase-linear time grid for elbow and
upper-arm/torso interior angles,21 points per phase, bottom once. No DTW claim.
Any missing required observation or excessive raw gap invalidates the pair. All
1×3 QMotion and1×6 IMSA pairs are shown; no best-pair selection. Components carry equal
weight; formula100×(1−mean absolute angular difference/180). Original-time pair
mappings and phase durations are retained. `valid_technique_score` stays null.

The displayed92-point values are a deliberately transparent **unvalidated angle-pattern
agreement**, not percentage-correct form. The180° normalizer is not a clinically or
biomechanically calibrated tolerance. This scale can hide meaningful differences,
and phase-normalizing a merged candidate can be misleading. Its purpose here is to
make the arithmetic reviewable, not establish the moat as solved.
