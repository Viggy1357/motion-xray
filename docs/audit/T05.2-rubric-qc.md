## Defects found and repairs applied

1. **S2 inventory URL wrong.** Draft inventory linked NASM to an NSCA practice-questions URL while the references section had the correct NASM URL. Repaired: inventory now uses the actual NASM blog URL.
2. **Overclaimed arm–torso angle.** M4 was described as distinguishing a lat-driven path. A scalar side-projection angle cannot distinguish out-of-plane motion or prove lat-driven path, lat activation, or pulldown-vs-row. Repaired: observable limit stated; M4 is a proxy only.
3. **Torso lean baseline underspecified.** M1 lacked a camera-vertical/world baseline and view-eligibility requirement; seat-back normal is not gravity-aligned. Repaired: baseline convention, labeling, and view eligibility required for absolute lean.
4. **Excursion over-constrained.** M2 was made dependent on full calibration and both-side landmarks. Repaired: within-camera dynamic excursion is computable with a stable camera and valid signed plane without physical gravity; visible-side validated landmarks suffice; side-projection limitations retained.
5. **Implementation status overstated.** M3/M6 are raw per-frame angles only; per-rep ROM and endpoint events are not implemented. M7 has an experimental trial with a known defect (merges bottom-threshold entry, not the extremum). Repaired in the matrix.
6. **Contract mischaracterized.** Draft implied the pose contract forbids head joints. PoseFrame uses an arbitrary joint-name dictionary; the true gap is no implemented, validated head metric. Repaired in M8.
7. **Wrist proxy scored as bar.** Draft allowed wrist proxy to stand in for bar endpoint. Repaired: proxy cannot establish chest contact or acceptable bar landing and must not be scored as bar.
8. **Coverage logic too weak.** Draft allowed either torso metric and let "marked unavailable with reason" still permit an aggregate. Repaired: both M1 and M2 required; any missing required core metric makes the aggregate unavailable; components-only reporting otherwise.
9. **Double-count risk.** Elbow ROM and return extent are now one component family, not independent weighted features.
10. **Tempo nuance.** Draft implied all sources demand a slow concentric. ACE says "slowly exhale" without an explicit slow-pull prescription; NASM control-both-directions is actual. Tempo is separate and not required for the posture score.
11. **Caption fidelity.** Jeff's wording is "pretty straight line" in side view (not "roughly straight"); selection limited to 03:10–04:45, auto-captions, not manually audio-verified. Repaired.
12. **Threshold discipline.** ACE 30° and Jeff top-view 10–15° are cues, not auto score thresholds; 2–3 demo clips are a calibration range, not validated tolerances or population rules. No new ranges, tolerances, or weights introduced.
